Privacy Policy

Personal Data Protection Notice issued in accordance with the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL, Royal Decree No. M/19) and the regulations of the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

1. Data Controller Statement

1. Legal Framework & Purpose

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data collected through davidgroeve.com and associated portal services. Operations strictly adhere to the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) issued under Royal Decree No. M/19 and its Executive Regulations enforced by SDAIA, as well as applicable guidelines from the Digital Government Authority (DGA) and the Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST).

2. Data Collection & Processing

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We collect and process personal data necessary for executive consultation, portal authentication, and administrative interactions:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Name, professional email address, phone number, organization, and country of operation.
  • Portal Credentials: Authentication data managed securely through encrypted tokenization (Supabase Infrastructure).
  • Technical & System Logs: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, session timestamps, and diagnostic telemetry for system integrity and cybersecurity auditing.
3. Data Sovereignty & Localization

3. Data Storage & Sovereign Localization

In alignment with national cloud security standards established by the National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) and SAMA cybersecurity frameworks, all sensitive executive records, customer logs, and portal credentials processed within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia comply with sovereign data residency principles. Cross-border transfers occur strictly under compliant mechanisms permitted by SDAIA.

4. Rights of Data Subjects

4. Your Rights under KSA PDPL

Under Article 4 of the Saudi PDPL, data subjects residing in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia hold the following rights:

  • Right to Know: The right to be informed regarding the legal basis and purpose of personal data collection.
  • Right of Access: The right to access and obtain a copy of held personal data in a readable format.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to request correction or updating of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Right to Destruction: The right to request the erasure of personal data when processing is no longer legally necessary.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: The right to withdraw processing consent at any time, subject to statutory exceptions.
5. Cybersecurity & Retention

5. Security Safeguards & Retention Periods

We employ multi-layered technical safeguards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, stringent role-based access control (RBAC), and continuous threat monitoring. Personal data is retained strictly for the duration necessary to fulfill operational, legal, and regulatory requirements before automated cryptographic erasure.
